Reading the dates

Years ago / BP is used for deep prehistory (“before present”, conventionally 1950). Subtract about 2,000 to convert to BCE: 12,000 years ago ≈ 10,000 BCE. cal means a radiocarbon date calibrated to calendar years. Palaeolithic dates often carry wide error ranges and sometimes conflict between methods, as at Kaldar Cave.

What counts as “Iran” here

Events from neighbouring Mesopotamia and Anatolia appear as Wider Middle East where they shaped Iran. Map pins mark the best-known site for each entry; empire-wide or climatic events are pinned to a representative location, and the trade-route overlays are illustrative and approximate.

Sources & caveats

For the Median and early Achaemenid periods much of the narrative comes from Greek writers, especially Herodotus, writing a century or more later. Where royal inscriptions and Greek accounts disagree, the entry is marked debated. New excavations regularly revise prehistoric dates.
