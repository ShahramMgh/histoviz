The Iranian Heritage100,000 years of human evidence — from Neanderthal caves in the Zagros to the fall of the Sasanians, and on to 1979

A sourced atlas of what archaeology, inscriptions and ancient writers reveal about the people of the Iranian plateau and its borderlands — the Zagros, Susiana, the Caspian shore, Sistan and Kerman. Scrub the years to watch the region’s kingdoms redraw the map, and open any moment for its full story, photographs and sources.

What’s inside

One interactive atlas of the Iranian plateau

Timeline & map, side by side

A horizontal timeline of 200+ moments — from Palaeolithic caves to the 20th century — linked to a map of the plateau. Pick an era to move both at once, or filter by theme, UNESCO status and more.

The region through time

A year slider redraws the political map of the whole region from real historical borders — Elam, Assyria, the Achaemenids, Parthia, the Sasanians, the caliphates and beyond — kingdom by kingdom.

A page for everything

Every site, ruler, poet, philosopher and kingdom opens a dedicated page with a detailed article, references, and a photo archive drawn from Wikimedia Commons.
